Why two sine modes are perpendicular

The product of sin(πx) and sin(2πx) is positive over some stretches and negative over others. The two areas are equal, so they cancel: the integral is zero.

sin(πx) sin(2πx) product
positive area negative area

The product curve sits above the axis where the two modes share a sign and below where they differ. Summing it across the rod, the shaded regions above and below the axis exactly offset, which is what the integral evaluating to zero means.
