**Turning quant models into reliable production systems**

I help trading desks and quant teams translate complex financial models into practical infrastructure — from derivatives pricing and risk analytics to quantitative investment strategy (QIS) systems.

## What I Do

Many strong quant ideas fail to deliver value because the implementation is fragile, difficult to scale, or disconnected from real trading workflows.

My work focuses on closing that gap — translating quant models into robust systems used in daily production.

**Selected work:**
- Building cross-asset QIS production infrastructure  
- Pricing linear and non-linear oil derivatives  
- Designing and implementing IR risk hedging strategies for energy desks  
- Developing intraday risk and P&L attribution tools for commodity trading  
- Implementing hourly power swaps in Openlink  
- Modeling counterparty credit risk for commodity derivatives
